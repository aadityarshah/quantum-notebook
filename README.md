# QIST-Notes

This repository contains notes made by me based upon the contents learned in the courses *PH202 Introduction to Quantum Physics, PH643 Quantum Computing and Information, and MSE318 Quantum Materials and Devices*.   

The aim is to understand Quantum Information Science and Technology (QIST) properly at a foundational level as much as possible. 

## Repository Structure 

```text
qist-notes/
├── assets/
├── qist-combined-notes/
├── quantum-computing-and-information-notes/
├── quantum-materials-and-devices-notes/
├── quantum-mechanics-notes/
├── README.md
```
The contents in `qist-combined-notes` directory are based upon the contents learned overall from all the courses by trying to correlate the important and connecting information from them.  Other directories contain the notes based upon what was learnt from the lectures.
